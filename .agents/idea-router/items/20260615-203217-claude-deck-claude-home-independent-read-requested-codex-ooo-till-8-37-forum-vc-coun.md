## The 5 flagged questions — my answers
**Q1 (MFN scope):** OVERSTATED. §7(g) MFN is literally scoped to convertible NOTES, not SAFEs — so "we can't issue below TEDCO's $5M cap" is NOT automatic. Your real protection is §4(g)(xii) ("diminish/circumvent" + consent), which is softer and fact-dependent. Don't build the pitch on the $5M-cap-floor argument; it's arguable, and a sophisticated counterparty's counsel will say so.

**Q2 (the $600K match):** THE THINNEST POINT. §4(g)(xiv) grants TEDCO the RIGHT to participate pari-passu — it does NOT commit a dollar amount, and a state instrumentality follow-on requires committee approval on TEDCO's timeline (weeks/cycles), not tomorrow. "Founder states ~$1.5M capacity" is a belief, not a commitment. So "$1.2M raised for 6%" conflates COMMITTED ($600K Forum) with HOPED-FOR ($600K TEDCO, later, uncontrolled). The headline number is not real money yet.

**Q3 (does Forum deploy $645K at $20M to keep its fee?):** PROBABLY NOT — you answered your own question. The accelerator economics REQUIRE the cheap $1.933M cap; that's how the $45K fee earns out (cheap equity offsets program cost). At a $20M cap the fee is pure cost to Forum with no cheap-equity upside. Option 2 is likely a STRUCTURAL non-offer — Forum can't say yes to it.

**Q4 (structural/tax/securities):** The biggest one isn't tax — it's the **valuation comp**. Option 1 sells $100K at a $5M cap to the SAME counterparty you're anchoring at $20M pre. You just handed Forum (and every future investor who hears it) the comp "Sirsi sold equity at ~$5M last week." That poisons the $20M anchor for the main raise. A 4x price gap to the same buyer in the same week reads as "the $20M is soft." This is the strategy actively undermining its own anchor.

**Q5 (hard take-it-or-leave-it w/ a fund you want follow-on from):** WRONG TONE for Option 2. Ultimatums are fine for a small clean check (Option 1 — who cares if they walk). But a $500K fund check is the start of a multi-round RELATIONSHIP; boxing them on entry trades relationship/signaling/network capital for ~1% equity. Also: presenting TWO options is a MENU, not take-it-or-leave-it — it invites a counter-proposed THIRD option. "Pick one of my two only-terms, then silence" is internally contradictory.

## The structural error under all of it
You're reading §4(g)(xiv) as a WEAPON ("triggers TEDCO's match = more money"). It's a RESTRICTIVE COVENANT ON YOU: "Company shall not accept >$250K without OFFERING TEDCO a slot." It's a BRAKE on your freedom to take Forum's $600K cleanly/fast, not an accelerator. Crossing $250K obligates you to pause and offer TEDCO in — which can SLOW the round and hand a state committee a say in your timing. The polarity is backwards.

## What I'd do instead (go-forward)
1. **Keep the stance, drop the syndicate.** Founder-favorable + walk-away is right (you're at $182K rev, zero dilution — leverage is real). But leverage only works behind CLEAN terms, not an over-engineered structure on assumptions you don't control.
2. **ONE option, not two.** Decline the accelerator program entirely (the fee + cheap $1.9M cap IS the bad part — there's no version of it that doesn't comp you down). Offer Forum's FUND a $250–500K check at the $20M round cap. Clean, no fee, no $5M self-comp, no §4(g)(xiv) drama if you keep the single instrument at/under the threshold or loop TEDCO early.
3. **Use TEDCO as VALIDATION, not as a mechanical match.** "A Maryland state fund underwrote us and has pari-passu rights" is a strong SIGNAL to Forum. Don't convert it into a load-bearing $600K assumption you can't deliver.
4. **On §4(g):** if any single instrument goes >$250K, proactively loop TEDCO BEFORE signing — §4(g)(xii) (circumvention + consent) has the real teeth, and getting caught structuring around it is the one move that turns a friendly state fund adversarial. Stay clean.
