Three Surfaces

Use it the way you work

One binary, three ways in. Each surface tells you the same truth about your machine.

❯_

Command Line

the primary surface · scriptable

Every command stands alone: sirsi scan, sirsi ghosts, sirsi diagnose. Each one ends with a plain-English summary and what to do next. Add --json and pipe it anywhere.

☥

Menu Bar

always on · macOS

An ankh in your menu bar keeps watch: green when the machine is clean, amber when there's waste worth reclaiming, red when memory is under real pressure. Click it for the details.

𓂀

Horus Dashboard

local web app · for power users

sirsi dashboard opens a live view at localhost:9119 — findings, health, and history streaming in one place. It runs on your machine and only your machine.

The Pantheon

Every job has a name

Pantheon is built as a house of specialists. Each deity does one thing well, and they all report what they find in plain English.

𓁢

Anubis

The Scanner

Finds the waste — caches, build leftovers, forgotten downloads — with 81 rules and a reason for each finding.

𓂓

Ka

The Ghost Hunter

Finds what uninstalled apps left behind: stray settings, launch entries, and caches for software that's already gone.

𓂀

Horus

The Watcher

Sees the whole machine — every scan, alert, and diagnosis gathered into one local dashboard.

𓆄

Ma'at

The Standard

Holds work to a standard before it ships — test coverage, honest docs, and quality gates for your repos.

𓁟

Thoth

The Memory

Remembers your projects so AI assistants don't start from zero every session — and read far less to catch up.

𓁐

Isis

The Guardian

Hardens your network and watches system health — and automatically undoes any fix that breaks something, within seconds.

𓇽

Seba

The Surveyor

Knows your hardware, chip to memory, so every recommendation fits the machine it runs on.

𓇳

Ra fleet · in development

The Fleet Lord

Extends everything above from one machine to a whole fleet — the same rules, across every node you run.

Built to be Trusted

Your machine. Your data. Your call.

A tool that touches your files has to earn the right to. These aren't settings — they're how Pantheon is built.

𓉐

100% local

Everything runs on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no data leaving the house.

𓁹

Zero telemetry

Pantheon phones no one. There is no analytics endpoint to opt out of — it doesn't exist.

𓊽

Trash-first

Nothing is deleted outright. Cleanups preview first, then move items to the Trash — your undo button.

𓂋

Open source

Apache-2.0, built in Go, developed in public — mistakes and all. Read every line.
