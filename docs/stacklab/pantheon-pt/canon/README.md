# Pantheon PT — Stack Lab canon

This directory is the authoritative Pantheon PT Stack Lab canon. It records
source, release, package, cask, remote-promotion, native-operation and custody
boundaries. It does not claim signing, notarization, cask installation,
installed-host qualification or whole-wing certification that has not been
evidenced.

## Process and diagram canon

- [`DIAGRAM_INDEX.md`](DIAGRAM_INDEX.md) is the process denominator and links
  each stable process ID to requirements, stories, source, evidence, owner,
  implementation state and the applicable Mermaid views.
- [`DIAGRAMS_RUNTIME_NATIVE.md`](DIAGRAMS_RUNTIME_NATIVE.md) covers the engine,
  native app, workspace and observation processes.
- [`DIAGRAMS_ROUTER_MAINTENANCE.md`](DIAGRAMS_ROUTER_MAINTENANCE.md) covers
  router workers, MCP, evidence custody, pressure and relay maintenance.
- [`DIAGRAMS_RELEASE_EVIDENCE.md`](DIAGRAMS_RELEASE_EVIDENCE.md) covers
  package, updater, cask, publication, install/rollback and evidence flows.
- [`DIAGRAMS_MAAT_STACKLAB_LOCAL.md`](DIAGRAMS_MAAT_STACKLAB_LOCAL.md) covers
  Ma’at archive commands and local Stack Lab preference/catalog workflows
  PT-P29–PT-P40.
- [`TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md`](TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md) remains the requirement to
  evidence record; diagram presence is structural documentation, not test,
  qualification, release or certification evidence.


## Start here and required canon

[Certification](CERTIFICATION.md) separates verified wing identity and public140
evidence from final composed141 acceptance. The nine required records are
[PRD](PRD.md), [Plan](PLAN.md), [User stories](USER_STORIES.md),
[Security](SECURITY_THREAT_MODEL.md), [Design](DESIGN.md), [Runbook](RUNBOOK.md),
[Changelog](CHANGELOG.md), [Traceability](TRACEABILITY_MATRIX.md) and
[Component catalog](COMPONENT_CATALOG.md).

[Deities and modules](DEITY_AND_MODULE_MAP.md) and the
[exact module inventory](MODULE_INVENTORY.json) expose source, dependency and
replacement boundaries. The remaining full-census diagrams are
[Ma’at governance](DIAGRAMS_MAAT_GOVERNANCE.md),
[Ra/Horus operations](DIAGRAMS_FABRIC_OPERATIONS.md) and
[product operations](DIAGRAMS_PRODUCT_OPERATIONS.md). The84-group index covers
the finite independent census; diagram counts are not installed acceptance.
The inventory is regenerated from the committed141 composition named in
CERTIFICATION. Final-head digest equality, publication and installed evidence
remain separate acceptance steps.

The [Ma’at source lifecycle](MAAT_SOURCE_LIFECYCLE.md) records the bounded
receipt, replay, owner-adoption, custody, and qualification limits. It is
source-level contract documentation, not evidence of empirical calibration,
installed acceptance, or gate activation.
