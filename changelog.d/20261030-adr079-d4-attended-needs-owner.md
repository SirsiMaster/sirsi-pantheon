### Fixed
- **An attended lane whose session has closed now reports needs-owner, not armed (ADR-079 D4).** A declared-attended lane's wake-loop always holds dispatch, yet it still published `ConsumerCapable` on its own thread. So `WakePass`, `doctor` and `ctr` counted the lane as armed after its interactive session closed, and nobody was told. The loop no longer claims capability for an attended lane, on both of its publication paths. `session-message` lanes with no live session now report `needs-owner: … cannot be resurrected locally` instead of "unsupported wake mechanism". This adds the close-session negative fixture ADR-079 §6 requires. Refs: ADR-079.
