### Fixed
- Router send-quota: a verified, one-time reply to an inbound item no longer counts against the replier's own `MaxSendsPerSenderPerWindow` ceiling. `routerstore.isVerifiedReplyTx` exempts a send only when the cited `SourceItem` is a real item addressed TO the sender and sent BY the recipient (DB-verified, not caller-claimed), and only once per source item — re-citing an already-used source item, or any unprompted send, still hits the flat ceiling. Fixes the case where answering a review storm throttled the victim of the storm instead of its source. Refs: ADR-035 §2b axioms 4-7; ledger task `ssa-reply-quota-exemption`.
