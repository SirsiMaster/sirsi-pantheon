## Direction contract

THESIS: Pantheon is a calm, native operations room, not a dashboard: the operator sees one truthful current state, one next action, and the evidence that closes it. It refuses the dense “everything is urgent” panel and the separate menu-bar mini-app.

OWN-WORLD: Black-first macOS workspace with a near-black field, graphite structural surfaces, restrained gold for Sirsi identity and consequential commit actions, and emerald only for current healthy/live/selected state. The canonical Sirsi mark is the sole product mark. Native SF typography, native controls, thin dividers, generous breathing room, and high-contrast data are the visual grammar.

STORY: An operator arrives, understands whether the estate is ready or needs attention, opens a real guided resolution, inspects Ma’at's evidence, composes a Stack Lab recipe, and follows it into Apollo’s measured telemetry. Every status resolves into a next action, owned escalation, or recorded acceptance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact identity rail at left carries the Sirsi mark, current workspace, and grouped destinations. The main canvas opens on a single large operational sentence with live state and a primary in-app action; a quiet evidence strip sits beside it. A short active-work queue and a two-column “Build with intent” route put Stack Lab and Apollo at equal prominence, without metric-card clutter. Consequential controls align at the top right; quiet navigation and secondary evidence remain subordinate.

FORM: Grounded candidate 6, “native black-field operator workspace,” selected by the user’s explicit black/emerald/gold, native-macOS, actual-logo, and no-dead-end constraints. Seed key: 10b19be5. A code-led session is used because the user requested a working native application rather than a design comp; no persistent build-path setting is inferred.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
